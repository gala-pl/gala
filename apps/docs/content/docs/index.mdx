  # Gala

  Gala is a standalone language for programs that mix classical control with quantum kernels.
  Its design puts quantum constraints in the language: qubits are linear resources, measurement
  crosses an explicit effect boundary, and reversible computations can be uncomputed safely.

## The central idea

Quantum programs are still programs: they allocate resources, call functions, branch on data,
and eventually produce classical results. Gala makes the boundary visible instead of hiding it
inside a library call.

- `pure` functions are classical and deterministic.
- `quantum` functions describe measurement-free quantum evolution.
- `prob` functions have crossed the measurement boundary and may produce stochastic results.
- `Qubit` and `Qubits<N>` are intended to be linear values: moving them is allowed, silently
  copying or dropping them is not.
